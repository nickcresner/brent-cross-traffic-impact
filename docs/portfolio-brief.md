# Portfolio brief

## Problem

The A41 Brent Cross flyover renewals reduce road capacity at a major interchange. Local residents need to understand the geographical spread of disruption, additional travel time and the reliability of appointment journeys.

## Intended users

Residents, local businesses, schools and people travelling to appointments. The analysis may also help local representatives ask informed questions about mitigation.

## Proposed outputs

- A dated restriction map, with published sources and clear work phases.
- A time-of-day map of observed deterioration relative to a matched baseline.
- A separately labelled estimate of the works' contribution, after adjustment and validation.
- Journey profiles: typical duration, bad-day duration and coverage.
- A future arrival-time planner with parking/walking allowances and optional alerts.

## Evidence standard

Every numeric result must identify its observation period, source, coverage, baseline and uncertainty. No modelled delay is published as an observed fact. Unknown coverage is visible. Historic congestion and current congestion are distinguished from estimated causal impact.

## Portfolio narrative

Document the transition from a local problem to a research question, source selection, cartographic decisions, data-quality checks, evaluation and user-facing decisions. Record personal research decisions and iterations as the work proceeds. Do not claim the starter implementation as completed field research.

## Success criteria

- Reproducible analysis from a permitted source extract.
- A credible baseline and independently tested comparison roads.
- Results validated against held-out periods and actual journeys.
- Accessible map with trustworthy timestamps and coverage.
- Arrival advice evaluated using prediction error and achieved on-time arrival rate.
